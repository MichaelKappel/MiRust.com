Single-Source Rule
One current TinyRustLM source authority is allowed. A second writable checkout
blocks build, test, release, and deployment. Consolidation is semantic and
test-driven; stale trees are never union-merged wholesale.

Clean-Slate Rule

Git history. Current source and production paths contain no legacy reader,
validator, schema, fallback, compatibility alias, migration, or accepted debt.

Evidence Boundary

campaign progress. Private UAI, workspace.uai, agent-file-handoff, credentials,
raw evidence, model bytes, prompts, source, tools, tests, receipts, staging, and
historical ZIPs are not public release members.
